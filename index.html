Hardware & Assistive Robotics
Voice-Controlled Wheelchair Prototype
Project Overview

Designed to empower individuals with severe motor disabilities. Rather than physical push-rims or manual joysticks, the system converts acoustic voice commands into motor actions.

Technical Implementation
  • Microcontroller: ESP32 32-bit dual-core processing voice signals and triggering digital outputs.
  • Motor Driver: L298N Dual H-bridge driver delivering high current to DC geared motors without damaging logic chips.
  • Movement Logic: Programmed routines for Forward, Backward, Pivot Left, Pivot Right, and fail-safe Stop states.
  • Hardware Wiring: Power isolation circuit between motor supply and microcontroller logic to prevent back-EMF resets.
